Evertally quest-guide factual catalogue

Generated by tools/build_quest_guide_data.py from the installed QuestieDB Forever
metadata. Static source corrections are folded into QuestieDB_Forever.toc.
Character-dependent dynamic corrections are identified separately; affected quests
are not assumed available solely from this offline catalogue.

Source: https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB
QuestieDB contributors: Muehe, TheCrux/BreakBB, Drejjmit, Dyaxler, Cheeq,
TechnoHunter, Yttrium, Logonz and all other QuestieDB contributors.
The source's GPLv3 copying conditions and historical-rights qualifications remain
applicable; see https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/blob/master/LICENSE.md and
the copy of GPL-3.0 distributed at ../Evertally_Map/LICENSE.txt.

Dungeon membership is factual association from the project's existing dungeon
catalogue. No authored Matty7500 Quest Guide, Zygor or other proprietary route
text, ordering or implementation is included. Routes are computed by Evertally's
own planner from availability, prerequisites, levels and reference coordinates.

Zone level ranges are from the project's existing world.json, derived from the
MIT-licensed OpenMap Levels.lua (openmap-world/openmap); its individual source
attributions are retained in THIRD_PARTY.md and shared/sources/openmap/Levels.lua.

Reference coordinates are possible locations, not proof a unit is spawned or a
quest is currently offered. A computed route is not a manually verified 1-60 run.
